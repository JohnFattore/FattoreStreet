# LLM Notebook

## Why

Alongside the [journal](journal.md), the [blog](README.md) carries short study topics that Claude
writes as the author learns, one per topic. Readers need to know who wrote what: presenting
AI-written notes as the author's own words would mislead them. (inferred)

## Requirements

### Notebook posts are credited to Claude
A notebook post is always shown with Claude as its author, never the site's author or a blank
byline. (inferred)

### Notebook posts are grouped under their own category
Readers who want the author's writing, or only the study notes, can tell them apart at a glance.
Every notebook post is filed under the LLM Notebook category automatically. (inferred)

### Notebook posts publish the same way as the journal
A second publishing path would be a second thing to maintain. Notebook posts follow every
requirement in [journal](journal.md): merged means published, dated by when they were written, and
never removed by an import. (inferred)
