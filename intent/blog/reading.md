# Reading

## Why

The [blog](README.md) is only useful if visitors can find a post and read it. A blog that leaks
drafts or loses its links breaks trust in the rest of the site.

## Requirements

### Anyone can read the blog without signing in
Public writing behind a sign-in wall would defeat its purpose. Listing and reading posts needs no
account.

### Only published posts are visible
A post with no publish date, or one in the future, is a draft. Showing it would publish unfinished
work.

### Each post has a stable, readable address
Links to a post get shared and bookmarked, so a post is reached by a short name taken from its
title, and that name doesn't change when the post is edited.

### Posts can be searched and filtered
As the number of posts grows, scrolling stops working. Visitors can search post text and narrow the
list by category or tag.

## Non-goals

- **Browsing categories and tags directly, for now.** Filtering the post list by them is enough
  today. A page listing them is deferred, not ruled out.
